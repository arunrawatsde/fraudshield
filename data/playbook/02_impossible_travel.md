# Impossible travel

## What it looks like
A card-present (in-store) payment happens in one country, and another card-present payment happens
in a different country shortly afterwards, faster than any person could travel between them. This
usually means the card was skimmed and cloned, and the copy is being used abroad.

## Red flags
- Two in-store payments in different countries less than about 3 hours apart.
- The foreign payment is for resellable goods (electronics, clothing, gift cards).
- Amount noticeably higher than the customer's usual spending.
- Sometimes a device that does not match the customer's usual phone.

## What is not impossible travel
Genuine travel leaves a trail: a flight or hotel booking first, then a gap of many hours before
the first foreign payment, then everyday purchases abroad (restaurants, transport, groceries) at
normal amounts on the customer's usual phone. Online payments to foreign merchants are also not
travel, because the customer does not need to be there.

## Recommended action
Clear impossible travel with resellable goods: block and reissue the card.
Country change with a plausible travel trail: approve, or manual review if amounts are unusual.
