# Card testing

## What it looks like
Fraudsters who buy stolen card numbers first check which ones still work. They run a burst of very
small online payments (usually under $5) at low-friction merchants such as streaming services or
digital gift-card shops, often several within a few minutes, from a single device the cardholder
has never used. If the small payments go through, a larger "cash-out" payment often follows.

## Red flags
- Three or more payments under $5 within one hour.
- All from one device that does not appear in the customer's history.
- Online channel, digital goods merchants (streaming, gift cards).
- A larger gift-card payment minutes after the small ones.
- The country may not match the customer's home country.

## What is not card testing
A customer with several genuine subscriptions can be billed small amounts on the same day, but
these come from known merchants, spread over hours, and usually from the customer's usual device.
A single small payment is never enough on its own.

## Recommended action
Confident card testing: block the card and the follow-up payments, then reissue the card.
Burst on the customer's own known device with no follow-up: manual review.
